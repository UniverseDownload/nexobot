# NexoBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

NexoBot may process Discord information that is necessary to provide its features, such as user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and information provided when using commands.

NexoBot does not sell personal information.

Information is only processed as necessary to operate, maintain, and improve NexoBot.

Data practices may change as new NexoBot features are added. This Privacy Policy may therefore be updated.

If stored information is no longer required, it may be deleted.
